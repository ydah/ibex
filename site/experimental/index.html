Experimental

EVIDENCE BEFORE PROMOTION

Research features keep visible boundaries.

Experimental surfaces require explicit activation, publish resource budgets and failure modes, and may change or remain outside the stable contract.

Bounded minimum repair

Optional insertion, deletion, and replacement search interprets tables without running actions, then replays one selected repair through the normal runtime.

Measured error UX evidence →

Shipped incremental CST

Syntax-only incremental CST sessions are shipped as Experimental. They conservatively reuse Green subtrees, suppress parser actions, and still run generated lexer actions.

Read the incremental trust boundary →

Unshipped generalized parsing

Generalized LR and GLR ambiguity handling are not shipped. IELR is a distinct shipped Preview algorithm and is not presented as generalized parsing.

Read the research decisions →

Hard budgets

Ambiguity, counterexample, repair, and parser-session work is bounded. Exhaustion is a first-class result or structured exception rather than an unreported fallback.

Inspect budget APIs →

Promotion policy

Promotion requires a public contract, representative use, adversarial tests, performance evidence, migration notes, and complete documentation.

Read the complete policy →

Adoption guidance

Pin versions, test budget exhaustion, and avoid making experimental output a persistent interchange contract unless its documentation explicitly says otherwise.

Experimental means shipped only when its documentation names an explicit risk boundary.
